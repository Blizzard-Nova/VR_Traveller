# VR Traveller

VR Traveller is a simulation project that takes you back in time to the peak era of ancient civilizations. Explore a civilization in virtual reality during its golden age, talk with its citizens, and learn about the famous sites you can later visit in real life.

The simulation was created for the tourism industry. A traveler is first immersed in a simulated version of the place as it once was, then visits the actual site with that context and curiosity in mind.

## Concept

1. **Step back in time.** Enter a VR reconstruction of a civilization at its height.
2. **Meet the citizens.** Interact with the people who live there. They share information about their world and point you toward famous sites worth visiting.
3. **Visit the real place.** Take what you learned to the actual location, where it is easier to picture what the ruins and landmarks once were.

## Available Environments

| Environment | Location | Era |
| ----------- | -------- | --- |
| Birka Viking Town | Sweden | Viking Age |
| Gandhara | Pakistan | Ancient Gandhara civilization |

More civilizations and sites are planned.

## Why VR for Tourism?

- Gives travelers context before they arrive, so ruins and landmarks are easier to interpret
- Makes history interactive and memorable through conversation with citizens
- Lets people explore sites that are remote, fragile, or heavily restricted without causing wear
- Helps visitors decide which sites to prioritize on a real trip
